Abstract: Weak-lensing magnification complements cosmic shear through its effects on galaxy fluxes, sizes, and number counts. We derive the optimal magnification statistics from galaxy properties and show that the optimal measurement can be decomposed into an optimal mean statistic and total number counts. The optimal mean statistic can be constructed from the residual distribution of a generalized fundamental plane (GFP), $F(i,\boldsymbol{X})$, which predicts galaxy size or, equivalently, flux from surface brightness $i$ and additional lensing-invariant properties $\boldsymbol{X}$.
