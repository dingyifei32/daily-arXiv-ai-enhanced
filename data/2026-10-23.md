Result: 固定 $\Sigma m_\nu$ 时：最弱先验 $\sigma(H_0)\simeq1.5$、更严先验 $\simeq1.3$、加 $A_s$ 先验 $\simeq0.7$ km/s/Mpc；放开 $\Sigma m_\nu$ 会削弱 $H_0$ 约束，但可得 $\Sigma m_\nu$ 的 95% CL 上限约 0.4 eV。验证显示修正复合模型下 $H_0$ 无偏恢复，早期暗能量模型下则有偏。

Conclusion: $r_d$-agnostic 方法能给出不依赖标准早期宇宙标定的 $H_0$ 约束，但其有效性依赖早期物理假设：对早期暗能量类模型不再无偏，因此应视为一致性检验而非完全模型无关的测量。

Abstract: We present a sound-horizon-agnostic forecast for constraints on the Hubble constant $H_0$ and the sum of the neutrino masses $Σm_ν$ from a combination of LSST-like galaxy weak lensing, Simons-Observatory-like CMB lensing, the completed DESI BAO program, and expanded supernova samples. Treating the sound horizon at baryon drag $r_d$ as a free parameter, the method provides a consistency test of $Λ$CDM without relying on the standard early-Universe calibration of $r_d$. The analysis incorporates realistic galaxy weak-lensing systematics, explicitly marginalizing over photometric-redshift calibration, multiplicative shear biases, intrinsic alignments, and baryonic feedback, and extends the CMB-lensing analysis to $L_{\max}=5000$. For fixed $Σm_ν$, we forecast uncertainties of $σ(H_0)\simeq 1.5$ km/s/Mpc with our least informative prior set, $σ(H_0)\simeq 1.3$ km/s/Mpc with more restrictive priors, and $σ(H_0)\simeq 0.7$ km/s/Mpc with an additional informative prior on the amplitude of primordial fluctuations $A_s$. Allowing $Σm_ν$ to vary weakens the $H_0$ constraints, while still yielding a forecast 95% CL upper limit of about 0.4 eV on $Σm_ν$. We also clarify the scope of the $r_d$-agnostic approach through validation tests using mock data from modified recombination and early dark energy models. The method recovers unbiased constraints on $H_0$ for the former but not the latter, highlighting its dependence on assumptions about early-Universe physics.
