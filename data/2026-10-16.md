### [1] [The impact of nonlinear Landau damping on cosmic ray streaming and the role of external turbulence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20695)

Motivation: 电离星际介质中常假设NLLD是阿尔芬波的主导阻尼机制，但缺乏直接检验。该阻尼决定宇宙线散射率与漂移速度，进而影响宇宙线反馈强度，因此需验证此假设。

Method: 采用流体-PIC模拟，将热离子和电子建模为带可选朗道闭合的流体，从而对比有/无NLLD的相同运行。分别测试单能宇宙线自生波情形与植入模拟外部湍流的高振幅阿尔芬波情形，并分析饱和漂移速度与波谱的关系。

Abstract: Cosmic rays (CRs) streaming faster than the Alfvén speed generate Alfvén waves that scatter them. The saturated wave amplitude is regulated by a damping mechanism. If the CR distribution is undersampled, a numerical instability corrupts the largest resonant scales: unphysical, unresolved pitch-angle gradients resonantly generate backward-propagating waves, driving an inverse cascade that should be absent from purely streaming-driven waves. While power-law CR distributions may permit stronger NLLD, our results indicate that NLLD is unlikely to be an intrinsic saturation mechanism of the streaming instability; instead, its effectiveness hinges on the interplay of streaming-generated modes with those of external origin.
